II

About Our Parish

Rev. Fr. John Smith

Fr. Smith has served this parish faithfully since his ordination, guiding the community with a deep devotion to the traditional liturgy and the teachings of the Church. He holds daily office hours for spiritual direction and confession.

Founded in memory of Pope St. Pius X, our parish has stood as a home for the Catholic faithful for over sixty years, holding fast to the timeless traditions of the Roman Rite while serving the needs of families in our community.
